💳 UPI payment

Customers can pay by UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm and others) while placing an order. Enter your UPI ID below. After paying, the customer enters the 12-digit UPI reference (UTR) number. You then check the payment in your UPI / bank app and tap "Payment received" on the order. Leave the UPI ID empty to turn UPI payment off.
